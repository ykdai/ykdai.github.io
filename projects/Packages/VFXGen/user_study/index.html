Evaluation of Generated Visual Effects

This study compares visual effects generated by different methods. All participants complete the same fixed set of 20 trials. Each trial presents a reference image, a text prompt, and four anonymized videos. Evaluate the results separately on the three criteria below.

Fixed question bank: 20 trialsCandidates per trial: 4Responses per trial: 3 separate choices

Evaluation criteria

Select the best result for each criterion separately. You may select the same or different videos across the three criteria.

  1. Overall video quality

    Evaluate the overall effect of the video itself, including visual quality and animation. Do not include its match to the reference image or text prompt in this criterion.

  2. Image consistency

    Evaluate how closely the video matches the reference image in subject, appearance, color, and visual style.

  3. Text consistency

    Evaluate how closely the video follows the content, attributes, and motion described in the text prompt.

Procedure

  1. Read the prompt, inspect the reference image, and watch all four videos. You may replay them individually or play them together.
  2. Candidates are labeled A–D with randomized positions. Drag a criterion onto a video, select A–D, or tap a criterion then a candidate. Selections appear below each video and can be reassigned. Multiple criteria may be assigned to the same video.
  3. After all three criteria are answered, the study advances automatically. You may return to revise answers. Responses are submitted automatically after the final trial.
  4. Switch between Chinese and English at the top right at any time. Your selections and video order will be retained.
