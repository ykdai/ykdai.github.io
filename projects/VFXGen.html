Procedural VFX Generation with AI Agents

1Nanyang Technological University (ntu.edu.sg)

2Adobe Systems (adobe.com)

User Study

Abstract

Visual effects (VFX) are essential to modern film and game production, yet existing generative approaches primarily synthesize flattened videos, offering limited editability and failing to produce the structured assets required by professional workflows. We present Procedural VFX Generation with AI Agents, a framework that generates editable 2D and 3D VFX as structured After Effects projects rather than flattened videos. Our system adopts a two-stage agentic architecture: a Planner converts a text prompt and a reference image into a structured VFX plan, and an Executor translates this plan into concrete operations and parameters that construct an executable project file. To overcome the scarcity of high-quality VFX project data, we introduce a hybrid training strategy that learns effect composition and timeline structure from online tutorials and VFX assets from other software, while learning executable operations and parameters from artist-authored After Effects projects. We further construct a benchmark of 200 test cases, each pairing a text prompt with a reference image, covering 120 movie effects across 20 categories and 80 game effects. Experiments show that our trained agent achieves performance comparable to strong commercial baselines such as Claude Code and Codex, while producing structured, editable project files that integrate directly into professional production pipelines.
